## 📊 **4 Complete Trading Analysis Modes**

### **🟢 NetGEX (OI) - Structural Analysis**
- **Data Source**: Open Interest (outstanding positions)
- **Analysis Type**: Structural support/resistance levels
- **Trading Focus**: Multi-day positioning and key levels
- **Best For**: Swing trading, identifying persistent levels

### **🔵 NetGEX (Volume) - Flow Analysis**  
- **Data Source**: Today's volume (trading activity)
- **Analysis Type**: Intraday sentiment and momentum
- **Trading Focus**: Following today's flow and bias
- **Best For**: Day trading, momentum strategies

### **🟡 NetVEX (OI) - Volatility Structure**

- **Trading Focus**: Vol expansion/contraction strategies

## 💡 **Advanced Volume Flow Strategies**

### **Flow Following (Volume-Based)**
1. **Identify Direction**: Heavy call volume = bullish flow
2. **Confirm Momentum**: Volume increasing throughout day
3. **Entry Strategy**: Join the flow with similar positions
4. **Exit Strategy**: Volume decline or flow reversal

### **Flow Exhaustion (Volume-Based)**
1. **Extreme Volume**: 5x+ normal volume at specific strikes
2. **Sentiment Extreme**: 90%+ puts or calls
3. **Contrarian Setup**: Prepare for reversal
4. **Timing**: Wait for volume decline confirmation

### **Flow vs Structure Divergence**
1. **Volume Says**: Heavy bearish flow (puts)
2. **OI Says**: Strong structural support
3. **Strategy**: Fade the volume, respect the structure
4. **Risk Management**: Tight stops, quick exits

## ⚠️ **Risk Management by Analysis Type**

### **Volume-Based Trading Risks**
- **Flow Reversal**: Volume patterns can change quickly
- **False Signals**: High volume doesn't guarantee direction
- **Exhaustion**: Extreme volume often leads to reversal
- **Mitigation**: Tight stops, quick exits, position sizing

### **OI-Based Trading Risks**
- **Level Breaks**: Structural levels can fail dramatically
- **Slow Signals**: OI changes lag price action
- **False Breakouts**: Test levels before real moves
- **Mitigation**: Wider stops, patience, confirmation
